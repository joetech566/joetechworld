Prepare your installation

Before you climb

  • Check roof access, weather, and fall protection.
  • Keep away from power lines; never mount a dish where it can contact them.
  • Secure the mast and check that the mount is plumb.
  • Confirm the correct LNB, cable, connectors, and receiver settings.
  • Use a signal meter or receiver readings to fine-tune safely.
Quick diagnosis

What is happening with your signal?

Choose the symptom for a practical first-pass checklist.

Common checks

Start with the receiver's signal-strength and signal-quality meters. Check cable connections and water ingress, inspect the dish for movement or obstruction, and verify that the selected satellite and transponder settings are correct.

Do not work at height in unsafe weather. Electrical and roof work may require a qualified technician.
